Services

Custom websites, SaaS and apps for businesses.

The same care that goes into PIQSYNC, QUEUE and FLOW, for your business: software for the way your work is really done.

What we build

Websites

Company sites and landing pages that load fast on any phone and read well in English, Spanish or both. Static and simple to host, with nothing loaded from other companies unless you need it.

SaaS

Web software your customers or staff sign in to: accounts and roles, payments through Stripe, emails and texts, reports and exports. Designed so one customer can become many.

Apps

iPhone and Android apps, and small desktop helpers for Mac and Windows: including apps that keep working without internet and catch up when they’re back online.

Connecting your tools

Card scanners over Bluetooth, cameras, payment providers, email and text services: the hardware and services you already use, working together.

How a project goes

  1. A conversation

    Tell us by email what you need. We’ll ask questions and tell you honestly whether we’re a good fit.

  2. A short written plan

    What we’ll build first, what it will cost and when you’ll see it, in plain words.

  3. Small steps you can try

    You use real pieces early, and what you learn shapes the next step.

  4. Launch, then look after it

    We put it live with you, and keep it working and improving after launch.
