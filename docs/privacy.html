Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QAVENTIQ LLC ("QAVENTIQ," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with qaventiq.com (the "Site").

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Site only. Our software products, including PIQSYNC, FLOW and QUEUE, are covered by their own privacy policies, which are provided with those products.

2. Information we collect

The Site does not use forms, user accounts, cookies, tracking technologies or analytics, and it does not load content from third-party services. We do not collect personal information through the Site.

3. Information you send us

If you contact us at support@qaventiq.com, we receive your name, your email address and the content of your message. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and to keep a record of our correspondence. We do not sell this information or share it for marketing purposes.

4. Hosting and technical information

The Site is hosted by GitHub, Inc. through GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may process technical information about each visit, such as your IP address, in order to deliver the pages and protect its service. GitHub's handling of this information is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Data retention

We keep correspondence only as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain reasonable business records, after which it is deleted.

6. Security

The Site is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We take reasonable measures to protect any correspondence we receive.

7. Children's privacy

The Site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Site.

8. Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold from your correspondence, or ask us to delete it, by writing to support@qaventiq.com.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
