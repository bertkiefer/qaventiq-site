How they work together

  1. 01 · At the schoolQUEUE

    Each student’s card is scanned before the photo, so every picture belongs to the right child.

  2. 02 · At the studioFLOW

    That night the studio’s computer sends the day’s photos to the cloud and checks each one arrived.

  3. 03 · In the cloudPIQSYNC

    Photos are matched to students, proofed and put in each family’s gallery; families order and the studio’s lab prints.

PIQSYNC, QUEUE and FLOW are in their first season, working with one studio. We aren’t taking new studios yet; write to us if you’d like to hear when we do.

How we work

The way we build

  1. Start with the real day

    Before we design a screen we learn how the work is really done, where, and what goes wrong. QUEUE works offline because most school gyms have no internet for tablets.

  2. Plain words

    Screens and messages say what will happen, in plain words. PIQSYNC’s family pages work in English and Spanish.

  3. Keep working

    Software that picks up where it stopped and checks its own work: QUEUE without internet, FLOW overnight.

  4. Private by default

    Collect what the job needs and no more. This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers.

  5. Tested before it ships

    Every change runs through automated tests before it reaches anyone.

  6. Stay after launch

    We keep fixing and improving what’s live, alongside the people who use it every day.
